# Sales Enablement Packager

> **Part of the [Product Marketing Toolkit](../../README.md).** Before you start, read whatever exists in `context/` (positioning, objections, proof library, and the launch tier). If those files are empty or missing, ask for the essentials instead of guessing. Label anything you infer as an assumption, and never invent statistics, customer names, or quotes: use a bracketed placeholder like `[sourced metric, n, date]` instead.

A systematic framework for creating, packaging, and delivering sales enablement assets that equip teams to sell effectively.

## Overview

This skill helps product marketers:
- Determine which assets to create by release tier
- Build effective enablement materials
- Organize assets for easy discoverability
- Deliver training that drives adoption
- Measure enablement effectiveness
- Maintain and update assets over time

## Core Principles

**Seller-Centric Design**
Build for how sellers actually work, not how we want them to work.

**Findability Over Completeness**
Better to have 5 assets sellers can find than 20 they can't.

**Actionable Over Comprehensive**
Sellers need quick answers, not encyclopedias.

**Continuous Iteration**
Enablement is never "done" - iterate based on usage and feedback.

**Cross-Functional Alignment**
Partner with sales leaders, training teams, and ops to ensure adoption.

## Enablement Asset Library

### Internal Assets (For Sales/CS Teams)

**1. Product Playbook**
*Purpose:* Central hub for all product/feature enablement

*Contents:*
- Product overview (1-2 pages max)
- Example use cases and "how-to" scenarios
- Demo video (3-5 minutes)
- Customer-facing slides or one-pager
- FAQ (anticipated questions)
- Knowledge base article links
- Competitive positioning
- Discovery questions
- Objection handling

*Format:* One hub in whatever the field uses (enablement platform, wiki, CRM)
*Audience:* Sales, CS, Onboarding, Partners
*Update Cadence:* As needed when product changes

**2. Pitch Deck**
*Purpose:* Visual aid for customer conversations

*Contents:*
- Value proposition slides
- Problem/solution narrative
- Product capabilities with UI/UX screenshots
- Use case examples with visuals
- Proof points and customer stories
- Pricing (if appropriate)
- Next steps/CTAs

*Format:* PowerPoint/Google Slides
*Audience:* AEs, CSMs for customer presentations
*Update Cadence:* Quarterly or with major releases

**3. One-Pagers (Multiple Types)**

**Product/Feature One-Pager**
- What it is (1-2 sentences)
- Key benefits (3-5 bullets)
- How it works (visual diagram)
- Who it's for (target customer)
- Proof point or customer quote

**Competitive One-Pager**
- vs. [Competitor] positioning
- Key differentiators
- When we win
- Trap-setting questions
- Proof points

**Vertical/Industry One-Pager**
- Industry-specific use cases
- Relevant pain points
- Tailored value props
- Industry proof points
- Vertical-specific messaging

*Format:* PDF, 1 page, visually designed
*Audience:* All customer-facing teams
*Update Cadence:* As needed, review quarterly

**4. Battlecards**
*Purpose:* Competitive positioning at-a-glance

*Contents:*
- Quick comparison (us vs. them)
- When you'll encounter them
- Why we win (3-5 points)
- Trap-setting questions
- Landmines (their weaknesses)
- Objection handling
- Proof points

*Format:* 1-2 pages, scannable format
*Audience:* Sales teams in competitive deals
*Update Cadence:* Quarterly or when competitor changes

**5. Discovery Question Guide**
*Purpose:* Uncover pain and qualify opportunities

*Contents:*
- Situation questions (understand current state)
- Problem questions (surface pain)
- Implication questions (amplify impact)
- Need-payoff questions (build desire)
- Organized by customer segment or use case

*Format:* Quick reference sheet or embedded in playbook
*Audience:* AEs, CSMs in discovery conversations
*Update Cadence:* Based on what's working in field

**6. Objection Handling Guide**
*Purpose:* Counter common pushbacks

*Contents:*
- Common objection (exact words)
- Response framework
- Redirect strategy
- Proof points to use
- When to bring in specialists

*Format:* Table or Q&A format
*Audience:* All customer-facing teams
*Update Cadence:* Based on emerging objections

**7. FAQ Document**
*Purpose:* Quick answers to common questions

*Contents:*
- Product functionality questions
- Pricing and packaging questions
- Technical requirements
- Implementation timeline
- Support and services
- Competitive questions

*Format:* Searchable document or knowledge base
*Audience:* All customer-facing teams, Partners
*Update Cadence:* Continuous updates as questions arise

**8. Demo Videos**

**Marketing Demo (External)**
- 2-3 minutes max
- Problem → solution → outcome
- Professional production
- Customer-facing, shareable

**Product Demo (Internal)**
- 5-10 minutes
- Feature walkthroughs
- How to demonstrate key capabilities
- Common demo flows
- Tips and tricks

*Format:* Video (MP4) hosted in enablement platform
*Audience:* Marketing demos for prospects; Product demos for internal teams
*Update Cadence:* When UI changes or new features launch

**9. Talk Tracks / Scripts**
*Purpose:* Language sellers can use verbatim

*Contents:*
- Opening statements
- Transition phrases
- Value prop articulation
- Closing statements
- Email templates
- Voicemail scripts

*Format:* Text document with copy-paste friendly formatting
*Audience:* All customer-facing teams
*Update Cadence:* Based on what's converting

**10. Success Stories / Case Studies**
*Purpose:* Proof that solution works

*Contents:*
- Customer background
- Challenge they faced
- Solution implemented
- Results achieved (with metrics)
- Quote from customer
- When to use this story

*Format:* 1-page PDF or slide
*Audience:* All customer-facing teams
*Update Cadence:* Add new stories quarterly

### External Assets (For Customers/Prospects)

**1. Customer-Facing Slides**
*Purpose:* Slides AEs can share with prospects

*Contents:*
- Product overview
- Value proposition
- Key capabilities
- Use case examples
- Proof points
- Clean, professional design

*Format:* PowerPoint/PDF
*Audience:* Prospects and customers
*Update Cadence:* Quarterly

**2. Solution Briefs**
*Purpose:* Standalone documents to share

*Contents:*
- Executive summary
- Business challenge
- Solution approach
- Key benefits
- Customer examples
- Next steps

*Format:* Professionally designed PDF
*Audience:* Decision-makers and influencers
*Update Cadence:* Annually or major releases

**3. ROI Calculator / Assessment**
*Purpose:* Quantify value for prospects

*Contents:*
- Input fields for customer data
- Calculation methodology
- Output: projected value
- Assumptions documented
- Shareable results

*Format:* Excel, Google Sheets, or web tool
*Audience:* Economic buyers
*Update Cadence:* Annually, validate assumptions quarterly

## Asset Creation Process

### Step 1: Align on Requirements

**With Sales Leadership:**
- What are biggest gaps in current enablement?
- What assets would most help close deals?
- What objections are teams facing?
- What questions keep coming up?

**With Product:**
- What's changing?
- What new capabilities are available?
- What are key use cases?
- What's the competitive angle?

**With Marketing:**
- What's the messaging?
- What proof points exist?
- What content is already created?
- What's the campaign strategy?

### Step 2: Prioritize Asset Development

**Must-Have (Core Enablement):**
- Playbook with overview, use cases, FAQ
- One-pager for quick reference
- Demo video showing how it works

**Should-Have (Enhanced Enablement):**
- Pitch deck for formal presentations
- Battlecards if competitive
- Discovery questions for qualification
- Objection handling if anticipated

**Nice-to-Have (Supplemental):**
- Multiple one-pagers (vertical-specific)
- ROI calculators
- Leave-behinds
- Additional demo variations

### Step 3: Build Assets

**Content Development:**
- Start with messaging foundation
- Work with design for visuals
- Get product for screenshots/demos
- Source proof points from customers
- Write in conversational language
- Keep it scannable and brief

**Review & Validate:**
- Sales leadership review
- Top performer feedback
- Product accuracy check
- Legal/compliance review (if needed)
- Pilot with small group

### Step 4: Organize for Findability

**Enablement Platform Structure:**
```
Product/Feature Name
├── Overview & Training
│   ├── Playbook (main hub)
│   ├── Training recording
│   └── Quick start guide
├── Sales Assets
│   ├── Pitch deck
│   ├── One-pagers
│   └── Demo videos
├── Competitive
│   ├── Battlecards
│   └── Competitive one-pagers
├── Discovery & Qualification
│   ├── Discovery questions
│   ├── Objection handling
│   └── Talk tracks
└── Customer Resources
    ├── Customer-facing slides
    ├── Solution briefs
    └── Case studies
```

**Naming Conventions:**
- Include product/feature name
- Include asset type
- Include date (for versioning)
- Example: "Reporting_Playbook_2026-Q2.pdf"

**Tagging Strategy:**
- Tag by product
- Tag by customer segment
- Tag by sales motion (prospecting, upsell, renewal)
- Tag by content type

### Step 5: Deliver Training

**Live Training Session:**
- What's launching and why it matters
- Key messaging and value props
- Demo walkthrough
- How to use enablement assets
- Q&A
- Record for those who can't attend

**Async Training:**
- Recorded video (15-20 min max)
- Written summary (1-2 pages)
- Links to all assets
- FAQ document
- Quiz or knowledge check (optional)

**Reinforcement:**
- Follow-up email with key points
- Office hours for questions
- Manager toolkit for coaching
- Sales plays/examples
- Ongoing support channel

### Step 6: Measure & Iterate

**Track Usage:**
- Asset downloads/views
- Training completion rates
- Time spent on each asset
- Search queries (what are they looking for?)

**Gather Feedback:**
- Seller surveys
- Manager feedback
- Win/loss analysis
- Sales call reviews (Gong, etc.)

**Update Continuously:**
- Fix errors or outdated info immediately
- Update proof points quarterly
- Refresh competitive intel as needed
- Archive outdated content

## Training Delivery Frameworks

### Live Training Structure (1 hour)

**00:00-00:10 - Context & Why It Matters**
- What's launching
- Market opportunity
- Why this matters to sellers

**00:10-00:25 - Product & Positioning**
- What it is and how it works
- Key messaging and value props
- Demo walkthrough
- Use cases

**00:25-00:40 - How to Sell**
- Discovery questions to ask
- Objections you'll hear
- Competitive positioning
- Sales plays

**00:40-00:50 - Enablement Assets**
- Where to find everything
- What each asset is for
- Quick tour of playbook

**00:50-01:00 - Q&A**
- Open questions
- Next steps
- Support channels

### Async Training Structure

**Video Module (15-20 min):**
- Section 1: Overview (3-5 min)
- Section 2: How to position (5-7 min)
- Section 3: How to demo (3-5 min)
- Section 4: Resources available (2-3 min)

**Written Companion:**
- TL;DR summary (bullets)
- Key messages
- Discovery questions
- Objection handling
- Links to all assets

**Knowledge Check:**
- 5-10 questions
- Scenario-based
- Correct answers explained

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Too Many Assets**
❌ 50 different documents for one feature
✅ Core playbook + 3-5 supporting assets

**Not Seller-Tested**
❌ Launching assets without seller input
✅ Pilot with top performers, iterate based on feedback

**Buried in Folders**
❌ "It's in the drive somewhere"
✅ Clear structure, easy search, promoted regularly

**Training Without Reinforcement**
❌ One-and-done training session
✅ Ongoing support, manager coaching, best practice sharing

**Outdated Content**
❌ Assets from 2 years ago still in system
✅ Regular reviews, archive old content, version control

**No Measurement**
❌ Can't tell if anyone's using it
✅ Track usage, gather feedback, iterate

## Best Practices

**Start With Messaging Foundation**
Don't create assets before messaging is locked. Rework is expensive.

**Less is More**
Fewer high-quality assets beat many mediocre ones. Focus on core essentials.

**Design Matters**
Professional design signals quality and importance. Invest in good design.

**Make It Findable**
If sellers can't find it in 30 seconds, it doesn't exist to them.

**Use Seller Language**
Write how sellers talk, not how product teams write specs.

**Show, Don't Just Tell**
Screenshots, videos, and examples are more effective than paragraphs.

**Enable the Enablers**
Train managers and specialists first. They multiply your impact.

**Celebrate Wins**
Share success stories of sellers using assets to close deals.

## Integration with Launch Plan

**Pre-Launch (T-30 to T-0):**
- Develop all required assets
- Conduct live training
- Distribute async training
- Set up manager toolkits
- Prepare support channels

**Launch (T-0):**
- Send launch communication
- Make assets available
- Activate support channels
- Monitor questions/issues

**Post-Launch (T+7 to T+30):**
- Office hours for Q&A
- Gather initial feedback
- Share early wins
- Update FAQs based on questions
- Refine assets based on usage

**Ongoing:**
- Monthly asset reviews
- Quarterly deep-dive with sales leadership
- Continuous iteration based on feedback
- Archive outdated content
- Add new success stories
