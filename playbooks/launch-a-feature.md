# Playbook: launch a feature

The skills in the order a launch actually needs them.

| Step | Skill | Output | Human checkpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `launch-readiness` | Launch tier and phased plan | Agree on the tier. It sets the scope of everything after it. |
| 2 | `messaging-framework` | Layered messaging and positioning for the feature | Product and sales leads review |
| 3 | `claims-proof-audit` | Every claim in the messaging marked ship / fix / needs proof / cut | Get proof for anything marked "needs proof," or drop it |
| 4 | `positioning-review` | Review of the launch post or landing page draft | Apply the top 3 fixes |
| 5 | `sales-enablement` | Assets scaled to the tier | Run the training before launch day, not after |
| 6 | `launch-readiness` | Go/no-go check against the readiness checklist | Final call is a person's |

## Prompt to start

> We're launching [feature] on [date]. Read `context/`, then follow `playbooks/launch-a-feature.md`. Stop after each step so I can review before you continue.

## Tier shortcuts

- **Tier 3 (minor):** steps 1, 3, and a short changelog note. Skip the rest.
- **Tier 2:** all steps, lighter enablement.
- **Tier 1:** all steps, plus `competitive-intel` and `battlecard` if the launch changes how you compete.
