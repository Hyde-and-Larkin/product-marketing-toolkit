# Playbook: respond to a competitor move

A competitor launched, cut prices, or started showing up in more deals.

| Step | Skill | Output | Human checkpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `competitive-intel` | Sourced brief on the move and what it changes | Check the "open items for human review" section |
| 2 | `positioning-review` | Your current positioning reviewed against the new landscape | Decide whether positioning changes or just the talk track |
| 3 | `battlecard` | Updated card for this competitor | Sales lead sanity-checks the traps |
| 4 | `discovery-questions` | Trap-setting questions tied to sourced weaknesses | |
| 5 | `sales-enablement` | Short field update: what changed, what to say, what not to say | Ship within the week |

## Prompt to start

> [Competitor] just [what happened]. Read `context/`, then follow `playbooks/competitive-response.md`. Start with the competitive-intel brief and stop for my review.

## Rule of thumb

Most competitor moves change the talk track, not the positioning. Change positioning only if step 2 shows your core differentiator is no longer true.
