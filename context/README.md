# Context: your product marketing source of truth

Every skill in this toolkit reads from this folder first. Fill these in once and every agent, teammate, and tool works from the same facts.

This is the part most AI setups skip, and it's the part that matters most. A skill built on vague positioning produces vague output, faster.

| File | What goes in it | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `company.md` | What you sell, to whom, how you make money | Everything |
| `positioning.md` | Positioning statement, category, differentiators | Positioning review, messaging, battlecards |
| `icp.md` | Ideal customer profile and buyer personas | Discovery, messaging, enablement |
| `voice.md` | How you sound and words you never use | Anything that writes copy |
| `objections.md` | What prospects push back on, in their words | Discovery, enablement, battlecards |
| `proof-library.md` | Claims you can back up, with sources | Claims audit, messaging, everything customer-facing |
| `competitors.md` | Who you lose to, and what you know about them | Competitive intel, battlecards |

## How to fill them in

- **Start rough.** A half-filled file beats an empty one. Mark unknowns as `TBD` so agents know to ask.
- **Use customer words.** Pull language from sales calls, reviews, and support tickets, not your own pitch.
- **Date things.** Positioning drifts. Put a "last reviewed" date at the top of each file.
- **Keep it private.** If you fork this repo publicly, make a private copy for your filled-in context. These templates are public; your positioning shouldn't be.

You can also ask your agent to draft these for you: "Read our website and last ten call transcripts, then fill in `/context/icp.md` and `/context/objections.md`. Mark anything you inferred."
